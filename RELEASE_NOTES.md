Fixes for the new weapon tables
- Weapon types can be set again: ticking a type in a weapon's Types list, adding a custom type, and typing into the box all work. The same fix applies to weapon proficiencies on the Proficiencies page and to the other lists inside tables.
- Calculated cells in the tables (To hit, Bonus dmg, Additional damage, a weapon's proficiency, and a weapon attack's damage) now have a slightly shaded background, so it's easy to tell what you can edit from what's worked out for you.

Upgrading from 1.2.x? Your characters open as they were; see the 1.3.0 notes for the new weapons, wondrous items and feature effects.
