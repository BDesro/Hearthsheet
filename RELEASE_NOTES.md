Weapons, wondrous items and effects that do the math
- Weapons: a new Weapons table on Details > Equipment holds each weapon's types, ability, damage dice and type, and special effects. Give a weapon effects such as "Silvered" (+1 damage), "+1" (+1 to hit and damage) or "Flame" (2d6 fire), and its to-hit, bonus damage and additional damage are worked out for you.
- Attacks fill themselves in: every weapon adds an attack on the Combat page with its name, to-hit and full damage roll, e.g. "1d8 + 4 slashing + 2d6 fire". Add more attacks with the same weapon from the new Weapon column; Dice override covers a different roll such as two-handed 1d10.
- Weapon proficiency follows your Proficiencies page: add a Weapon entry such as "Simple, Martial" or "Longswords" and every matching weapon becomes proficient automatically.
- Weapon types and weapon proficiencies are picked from a list of the standard types and weapons, so spellings always match. You can still add your own types (a Katana, say), and they show up in every list.
- Wondrous items: a separate Wondrous items list on the Equipment page lets you set exactly what an item changes. A Cloak of Protection with ac +1 and save.all +1 raises your AC and saves while it's equipped, and stops the moment you unequip it. Wondrous items still appear in the sheet's Equipment box and in the Play inventory.
- Feature effects: features and traits can now carry permanent modifiers, such as a fighting style or a species bonus, which apply across the whole sheet.
- New modifier targets: damage, plus attack and damage by ability or weapon type (attack.melee, damage.str, damage.ranged and so on).

Also new
- A short Tigrisoft logo intro plays on launch; click to skip it.
- Hearthsheet opens maximized, and table headers wrap instead of being cut off.

Upgrading your characters
- Your existing characters open exactly as they were: attacks, items and features keep every value and work as before. To get the automatic calculations, add your weapons, wondrous items and feature effects in the new places, then remove the old attack or item so nothing counts twice.
- Characters saved by 1.3.0 use a newer format. If you use Hearthsheet on more than one computer, update them all: an older version won't open these characters and leaves them alone when it syncs instead of overwriting them.
