Toolbar, dark mode and dice rollers
- New: a toolbar with New character, Print, Sync now and Help, plus a menu for the rest.
- New: dark mode. Choose Match system, Light or Dark from the toolbar menu. The character sheet stays white so it prints the same.
- New: Roll on each attack in Play. Type your physical dice or let the app roll; a natural 20 doubles the damage dice, and the result goes in the session log.
- New: resources can have a roll, such as "1d10 + fighter level" for Second Wind. Use then opens a roll window and can heal you or give temporary HP. Set the roll and what it does in Details, Resources.
- New: hover a resource in Play to see its notes and roll.
- Changed: the Details tables show readable names, such as "Long rest" instead of "LongRest".
- Important: characters saved by this version can't be opened by 1.9 or earlier. Update Hearthsheet on every computer you sync with.
